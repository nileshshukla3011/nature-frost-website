# 2026-10-22 — Search engine improvements

## Added

- **FAQ page (`/faq`)** with 13 questions buyers ask: what IQF is, shelf life,
  preservatives, nutrition, products, pack sizes, private label, custom cuts,
  markets, export, capacity, location and how to get a quote. Answers are
  built from the site's own product, pack and contact data, and search
  engines get them as FAQPage structured data. Linked from the phone menu,
  the footer and the contact page.
- **Typical uses** and **storage and handling** on every product page, e.g.
  "Soups and chowders, salads and corn chaat" for sweet corn — more useful
  detail for buyers, and more for search engines to match.
- **Structured data**: WebSite (the site name Google shows in results), and
  the company's logo, image, sales contact and map link. Breadcrumbs on the
  brochure and contact pages.
- **Search Console and Bing verification**: paste the codes into
  `verification` in `lib/site.ts`. README explains the set-up, including
  submitting the sitemap and creating a Google Business Profile.

## Changed

- **Page titles carry the search terms buyers use**: "Nature Frost — IQF
  Frozen Fruits & Vegetables, Bihar, India", "IQF Frozen Sweet Corn Supplier
  in India", "Frozen Vegetables & Fruits Range", "Contact & Bulk Enquiries".
  Product page descriptions now include typical uses.
- **Faster first screen**: the facility photo is served as WebP in four
  widths (33–130 KB instead of a 197 KB JPEG for every screen), and the logo
  as a 120 px WebP (8 KB instead of a 30 KB PNG). Page speed is part of how
  Google ranks pages.
- **Brochure PDF metadata**, which Google shows for PDF results: the title is
  now "Nature Frost — Company Brochure: IQF Frozen Fruits & Vegetables" with
  a subject line, instead of the Canva author name and design IDs. The pages
  themselves are unchanged.

## Fixed

- The 404 page carried both "noindex" and "index, follow" robots tags. The
  site-wide tag is gone, so the 404 page is simply noindex.
- The logo link's accessible name ("Nature Frost — home") did not match the
  visible wordmark ("NatureFrost"), which breaks voice control.
